About FarmLink

Digital agriculture marketplace connecting farmers directly to buyers through technology.

Our Mission

FarmLink is a digital agriculture marketplace developed by GenZ Solutionist to connect farmers directly to buyers through technology. Our platform focuses on improving food accessibility, reducing post-harvest losses, and helping farmers reach larger markets across Ghana.

We are building a smarter and more efficient agricultural supply chain where households, restaurants, vendors, and businesses can conveniently access fresh farm produce directly from trusted local farmers.

How We Deliver

FarmLink connects buyers and farmers through a simple online ordering system. Farmers upload available produce, buyers place orders, and delivery is coordinated efficiently to ensure fresh vegetables reach customers quickly and safely.

Our goal is to make fresh food distribution easier, faster, and more transparent for everyone involved.
